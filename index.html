WebRTC minicall and chat

This is free unlim call and chat with screen sharing.

Just open index.html in the browser, grant permission for access to Mic and Camera.
Then share link or "Your ID" - other person can open link and video call with you.
Examples:
- IPhone support, fixed attachment
- universal version with call, chat and attachment
- monolit html
- separated css and js
